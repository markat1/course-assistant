## Interpretation: fail-fast black hole
# A dead worker refuses connections within milliseconds, so its gateway queue
# drains instantly and it looks least loaded; least-loaded placement then sends
# nearly all traffic to it. The gateway only clears readiness on the next metrics
# poll (up to 5 s; polling.py sets ready=False on refresh failure). With 8 clients
# failing in milliseconds, 735 of 800 requests were burnt inside that window and the
# run ended in 30 s instead of ~100 s. Fix planned: passive health check - on a
# connection error in dispatch, mark the worker not ready immediately so the next
# placement avoids it; prepare_worker re-warms it when it returns.
